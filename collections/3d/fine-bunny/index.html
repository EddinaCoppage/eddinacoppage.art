Rabbit

Medium: Mixed Media

Dimensions: 8" x 6" x 4"

Year: 2024

Fine Bunny represents a delicate exploration of form and texture through three-dimensional artistry. This piece captures the essence of innocence and playfulness while demonstrating sophisticated craftsmanship in mixed media. The work invites viewers to consider the relationship between childhood memories and contemporary artistic expression.
