Twist

Medium: Mixed Media

Dimensions: 10" x 8" x 6"

Year: 2024

Twist explores the dynamic interplay between movement and form through three-dimensional sculptural expression. This piece captures a sense of fluidity and energy, demonstrating how static materials can convey motion and transformation. The work invites contemplation on the nature of change and the beauty found in transitional moments.
