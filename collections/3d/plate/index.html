Plate

Medium: Ceramic

Dimensions: 12" diameter

Year: 2024

This ceramic plate explores the intersection of functionality and artistic expression through sculptural form. The piece challenges traditional notions of tableware by transforming an everyday object into a contemplative art piece. The work demonstrates mastery of ceramic techniques while maintaining an elegant simplicity.
