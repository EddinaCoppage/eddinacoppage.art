Ellionna, my beloved sister

Medium: 2D Art

Dimensions: Variable

Year: 2024

This 2D artwork explores themes of identity, emotion, and human connection through carefully rendered forms and expressive composition. The piece demonstrates technical skill while conveying deep personal narrative, creating a work that resonates on both visual and emotional levels. Through color, line, and form, this artwork invites viewers into a contemplative dialogue about art and experience.
