ABOUT

Eddina Coppage is a multidisciplinary artist whose work explores themes of identity, culture, and personal narrative through painting and mixed media. Her practice bridges traditional techniques with contemporary perspectives, creating vibrant compositions that speak to the complexities of modern life. Based in Texas, she draws inspiration from her surroundings and experiences to create art that resonates with diverse audiences.
